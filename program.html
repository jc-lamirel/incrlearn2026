About

How do you teach a system that never stops learning, on data that never stops changing?

Data streams drift, user behaviour changes and knowledge goes out of date, while the large models now deployed everywhere are trained once and expected to stay relevant. Learning continuously — integrating new information without forgetting what is already known and without retraining from scratch — has become a central problem in AI.

Since 2013, IncrLearn has gathered the ICDM community working on this problem, from incremental clustering, concept drift and novelty detection to continual learning for large language models and foundation models.

The 10th edition features:

  • Two invited talks, by Eirini Ntoutsi and Elena Mocanu;
  • Two contributed papers, on data-free merging of model updates and on parameter reuse for continual offline reinforcement learning;
  • An open discussion on the future of the series and the associated journal special issue.
